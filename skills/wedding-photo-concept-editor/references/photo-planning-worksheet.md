# Photo planning worksheet — optional reference

Use only when a detailed on-location photography plan is wanted. It is a worksheet, not an automated camera control program.

## Concept selection grid

For each proposed concept include: intended feeling; people actually available; setting and verified access; camera viewpoint and focal-length *suggestion*, not a requirement; light direction; costume/prop dependencies; composition and background; privacy/permission status; alternative if access/weather is unsuitable.

Avoid exact sunrise/sunset times, venue permissions, package prices, or travel duration without verified evidence. If a user asks for schedule-dependent golden-hour timing, ask for location and date, then use an authorized data source; otherwise state timing is to be checked.

## Shot list schema

| ID | Priority | Moment / subjects | Composition + action | Light / staging | Dependencies and consent | Accessible alternative | Backup / time status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Must | Couple portrait | Two people walking, candid medium shot | Soft indirect light | Couple availability and site permission unverified | Seated portrait if needed | Covered veranda if rain; time TBD |

Maintain ordering for ceremony moments but never require poses in a place where a photographer is prohibited. Respect guests who opt out of photos and participants' mobility, sensory needs, and personal boundaries.

## Photographer handoff checklist

- Objective and brand/style vocabulary without copying copyrighted photos.
- Must-have vs optional shots; list of moments not to interrupt.
- Access restrictions, contact roles **only if user has permission to share**, privacy boundaries.
- Timeline dependencies and arrival constraints; mark unknowns.
- Whether sample imagery is authorized and may be stored or forwarded.
- Final image uses (personal album, printed wall art, public social post, paid advertising), each with different release considerations.
- Approval owner, backup plan, and corrections process.

All scenes and suggestions are creative planning, not claims of actual wedding coverage.
